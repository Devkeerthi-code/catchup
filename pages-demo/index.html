How it works

Four steps. About a minute.

  1. 01

    Export the chat

    WhatsApp: Chat › More › Export chat › Without media

    Telegram, Slack, Discord and Instagram exports work too. Or just copy and paste.

  2. 02

    Drop it in

    A .txt, .zip or .json file, pasted text, or up to 10 screenshots. We detect the app and date format for you.

  3. 03

    We read it on your device

    Parsing and masking run in your browser. Only masked text from the range you pick is sent to be summarised.

  4. 04

    Get your catch-up

    A five-line TL;DR, then deadlines, decisions, links, files, polls and open questions, each with its source.

Privacy, plainly

What happens to your chat

Group chats hold other people's phone numbers and payment IDs. We built around that first.

Only the masked text of the date range you pick. Names are already "Member 1, Member 2", and phone numbers, emails and UPI IDs are replaced before the request is made. Photos, voice notes and files are never read.

No. The raw export stays in your browser's memory and is gone when you close the tab. The server summarises the masked text and keeps nothing, unless you choose "Save this catch-up", which stores the summary only.

Yes, on your screen only. The list that maps "Member 4" back to a name is kept on your device, so you can switch real names on without them ever being sent anywhere.

WhatsApp (Android and iPhone, .txt or .zip), Telegram (JSON), Slack and Discord (JSON exports), Instagram and Messenger (JSON from "Download your information"), pasted text in any of these formats, and screenshots, which are read on your device.

Every item shows the message it came from, and "Open in context" shows the messages around it. If a date was changed twice, you will see both and which one is newer.
